Three possible results

Green: genuine
The copy is real and shows which centre it was issued to.
Yellow: tampered
Someone changed the hidden data or the question paper.
Red: no data
The image was not created by this system, or it was compressed.

How it works

Three simple steps, no technical knowledge needed.

Step 1

Upload the paper

The exam admin uploads the question paper as a PNG image and lists the exam centres.

Step 2

Sign and hide

Each centre's details are signed with RSA-1024 and hidden invisibly inside the image pixels.

Step 3

Verify a leak

Upload a leaked copy. The system reveals the source centre and detects any forgery.
